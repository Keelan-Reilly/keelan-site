AI inference performance.

I’m an electronic engineer from Dublin with a background in digital hardware, embedded software, and electronics. I’m also a classical flautist and pianist, and a former principal player with the National Youth Orchestra of Ireland.

I’m currently working on performance engineering for real-time AI inference, with a particular interest in physical-AI workloads such as VLAs and world models. I’m interested in how modern inference workloads execute on GPUs, where their latency and memory bottlenecks come from, and how those characteristics can be used to evaluate and shape future accelerator architectures.

At ASML, I built an FPGA–Linux diagnostic and calibration platform for precision-motion hardware. The FPGA handled real-time data acquisition and deterministic processing, while Linux handled configuration, diagnostics, and live visualisation.

In Formula Student, I owned the electrical architecture of a 300 V electric race car from scratch, designed and brought up more than ten custom PCBs, and built and validated its 75-cell battery-management system.
